Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Plant Tracker mobile application ("App") developed by Natalie Yee-Joy ("Developer", "we", "us") handles your information.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is not transmitted to our servers:

  • Plant names, species, and photos
  • Watering and care event history
  • Chat messages with the AI assistant
  • App preferences and settings

2. Data Sent for AI Processing

When you use the AI analysis or chat features, the following data is sent to our cloud proxy server for processing:

  • Plant photos (for AI analysis)
  • Plant name, species, season, and last watered date (for context)
  • Chat messages (for AI conversation)

Our proxy server forwards this data to Google's Gemini API (paid tier) for processing. We do not store your photos or chat messages on our servers. Data is processed in transit and not retained after the response is returned.

3. How Google Handles Your Data

We use the paid tier of Google's Gemini API. Under Google's paid API terms:

  • Your photos and prompts are not used to train or improve Google's AI models.
  • Your inputs are not reviewed by humans.
  • Data is processed transiently and not retained, except for brief safety and compliance logging.
  • Google acts as a data processor under their Data Processing Addendum.

Your data may be processed in any country where Google operates facilities. For more information, see Google's Gemini API Terms.

4. AI-Generated Content

Responses from the AI (plant health assessments, care recommendations, chat replies) are generated by artificial intelligence. These are not professional advice and may be inaccurate. See our Terms of Use for full disclaimers.

5. Analytics and Crash Reporting

The App uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to collect:

  • Anonymous usage statistics (e.g., feature usage counts, screen views)
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data

This data is anonymous and does not include your plant photos, names, or personal information. It is used solely to improve app quality and fix bugs.

6. Subscriptions

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not collect or have access to your payment information. Subscription status is managed through Apple's StoreKit framework.

7. No Account Required

Plant Tracker does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or location.

8. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the legal bases for processing your data are:

  • Consent: You choose to send photos and messages for AI analysis by tapping "Analyze" or sending a chat message.
  • Contract performance: Processing is necessary to provide the AI analysis features you request.
  • Legitimate interests: Anonymous analytics to improve app quality and stability.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Since all plant data is stored locally on your device, you exercise these rights directly by deleting data within the App or by uninstalling it. For analytics data, contact us to request deletion.

10. Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Deleting individual plants within the App
  • Uninstalling the App (removes all local data)

Since we do not retain photos or chat messages on our servers, there is no server-side data to delete.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, contact us at supportplanttracker@gmail.com.
